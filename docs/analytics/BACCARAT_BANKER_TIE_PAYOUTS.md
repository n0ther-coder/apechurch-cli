The table lists 776 bets in descending order of RTP. It includes the 774
distinct whole-number ratios with `1 ≤ TIE ≤ BANKER ≤ 50`, the `BANKER`
single-lane bet, and the smallest whole-number ratio that pays the same
on BANKER and TIE (`180 BANKER 19 TIE`). Multiplying both parts of a bet
by the same factor preserves its theoretical payout multipliers.

- **RTP** is expected gross payout as a percentage of the total stake.
  **round(RTP)** rounds that percentage to the nearest whole percent.
- **EV** is expected profit per unit of stake. **VARIANCE** is the variance
  of the payout multiplier. All metrics exclude VRF fees and gas.
- **Win %** is the probability that gross payout exceeds the stake.
  A payout of `1×` returns the stake.
- **Payout BANKER** and **Payout TIE** are multiples of the total stake.
  A PLAYER result pays `0×` on every bet in the table.
- **max payout** identifies which outcome pays the most. **#** preserves
  the original row labels.

See [Baccarat Odds and Payouts](./BACCARAT_ANALYTICS.md) for the payout
formulas and probability model.
