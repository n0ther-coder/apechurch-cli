# CLI 36 migration

`--multiplier` is renamed to `--cutoff` for Glyde and Crash. It remains a
cashout configuration, separate from result thresholds.

Result multiplier arguments require a number with at most two decimals and
`x`, for example `--target-x 2x`. Bare `2`, `2.001x` and scientific notation
are rejected. The multiplier is gross payout divided by actual wager of the
latest settled game. A transaction containing split/survive repetitions is one
game: sum its matching payouts and wagers. Blackjack uses the entire concluded
hand, including splits, doubles and side stakes. Earlier transactions do not
enter this ratio.

Whole-number `%` thresholds use cumulative net PnL divided by cumulative
actual wager in their documented scope. With gross payout P, wager W and
additional costs C, `100%` means `(P − W − C) / W ≥ 1`, or `P ≥ 2 × W + C`.
This changes `--target-profit 100%` and `--retrace 50%` to session totals.
Their bare APE forms keep the latest-game net profit/loss meaning. Other
session profit/loss guards retain cumulative scope. Zero wager cannot satisfy
a ratio. Unknown costs cannot prove a net threshold.

The bot runtime preserves `latest_play` in summaries and resumed contexts.
Parents can distinguish the last settled game from aggregate child economics,
including native loops. Failed or pending transactions do not replace it.
Use actual settled accounting; do not reconstruct payouts from net PnL.

Review saved commands before restarting. Old argument spellings are not
compatibility aliases. Historical migration documents retain their original
release semantics; this document supersedes those threshold rules.
